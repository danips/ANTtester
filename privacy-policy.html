Privacy Policy for ANTtester

Effective date: 23 November 2026

ANTtester (also called ANT Tester, Android package com.quantrity.anttester) is an Android diagnostic application published by Quantrity. It helps users check their device's ANT/ANT+ hardware and software support, including compatible external USB adapters.

1. Information the app accesses

To provide diagnostic results, ANTtester may access the following technical information on your device:

  • Whether built-in ANT functionality and USB host functionality are supported.
  • ANT adapter availability, adapter identifiers, firmware versions, and connection or permission status.
  • Installation status and version information for specified ANT-related services and plugins, such as ANT Radio Service, ANT USB Service, and ANT+ Plugins.
  • Information about attached USB devices, including device ID, vendor ID, product ID, manufacturer and product names, device name, and version, where available.

These details are used to display diagnostic results on your device. ANTtester does not intentionally read USB serial numbers. It does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

2. Collection, use, and storage

ANTtester processes diagnostic information locally on your device to display ANT/ANT+ compatibility and troubleshooting results. The app does not send these diagnostic results or personal information to the developer or to a developer-operated server. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services in the reviewed application code.

Diagnostic results are handled temporarily while the app is running. ANTtester does not maintain a user account or a persistent diagnostic history. Android may retain ordinary system diagnostic logs under its own controls; ANTtester does not upload those logs.

3. Permissions and locally connected services

ANTtester uses ANT-related permissions and may request access to a compatible USB adapter or ANT hardware where required by Android. This access is used to check capabilities and, where supported, read firmware information. The app communicates locally with separately installed ANT Radio Service, ANT USB Service, and device ANT hardware interfaces as needed for diagnostics. These services are not part of ANTtester and may have their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing and external links

ANTtester does not sell user data or transmit diagnostic information to external servers or third-party advertising or analytics providers. Local communication with installed ANT services is limited to operating the requested diagnostic functions.

The app may provide links to Google Play to install, update, or manage separate ANT-related applications. If you choose to follow these links, Google Play and the relevant third-party applications operate under their own privacy policies. ANTtester does not control their data practices.

5. Security, retention, and deletion

ANTtester relies on Android's application isolation and permission controls when accessing device functionality. Because diagnostic results are processed locally and are not uploaded to the developer, there is no developer-held diagnostic dataset to retain or delete. Temporary diagnostic information is cleared when the app's process ends; uninstalling the app removes its app-managed data according to Android's normal behavior.

6. Children's privacy

ANTtester is a technical diagnostics utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if ANTtester changes its functionality or data-handling practices. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.

8. Developer and privacy contact

Developer/publisher: Quantrity. Project: ANTtester on GitHub.

For questions about this policy, you can contact the developer using the contact details on the ANTtester Google Play listing or open an inquiry in the project's GitHub issue tracker. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
